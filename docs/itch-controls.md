## Controls

**Keyboard (one player)**
- Move: WASD or arrow keys
- Light attack: J (tap for a 3-hit combo)
- Heavy attack: K
- Block: L (hold). Tap it just before a hit to **parry**
- Dodge roll: Shift or I (it cancels a swing, too)
- Sparta kick: O (breaks guards, sends people flying)
- Magic: U
- Jump: Space
- Pause: P or Enter (the game also pauses when you tab away)
- Esc: pause. From the pause, death or victory screen it goes back to character select
- R: when you're dead, rise at the last checkpoint. When paused or after winning, restart the stage
- M: music on/off · G: change the gore level

**Each hero uses the buttons differently**
- Rurik: sword combo, cleave, Sparta kick, roll, Firebolt.
- The Mage (Oryn): staff combo, K = Chain Lightning (press it up to three times: each strike hits harder and the third floors him), O = Force Blast (hold it to charge a stronger push), dodge = Blink teleport (in the air too), U = a stone wall that only stops enemies.
- The Rogue (Vexa): a 4-hit dagger combo, K = Viper Strike, O = crescent kick (down + O = sweep; nobody close = throwing knife), U = Widow Mine. Run at a teammate and jump to vault off him. In the air: U = Shuriken Fan, K = Falling Viper (from a jump or a double jump). Her precision hits put the Mark of Death on an enemy: the next hit from another player is a super critical.
- Kick a crate or a chest (O) and it skids down the lane and bursts on the first enemy it meets.
- Hit a swinging blade with an attack, or the Mage's Force Blast, and it whips back the other way through your enemies. It can't hurt you while it does.
- Finishers: get behind an enemy who's running away and press J, K or O. Each hero has their own three.

**Gamepad (Xbox layout)**
- Move: left stick or D-pad
- X: light · Y: heavy · A: jump · B: dodge
- RB: block / parry · RT: Sparta kick · LB or LT: magic
- Start: pause · Back/View: menu

**Two players on one computer**
Gamepads go out first: with one pad, player 2 gets it. With two pads, each player gets one.
With no pads, player 1 uses WASD + J K L I U O Space, with P to pause.
Player 2 uses the arrow keys and the number pad: 1 light, 2 heavy, 3 block, 0 dodge, 4 kick, 5 magic, 6 jump, Enter to pause.

**Online co-op**
One player hosts and the other joins. Both of you run the full game. If your partner's connection goes quiet, the game waits for them. After a few seconds, press R to carry on alone.
